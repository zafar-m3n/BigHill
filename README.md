# Big Hill Lanka Pvt Ltd

Official corporate website for **Big Hill Lanka Pvt Ltd**, live at [bighill.lk](https://bighill.lk/).

## About the Company

Established in 2019, Big Hill Lanka is a leading importer, exporter, and distributor in Sri Lanka's food industry. The company began by exporting premium spices and seafood, then expanded into importing high-quality Basmati rice from India and Pakistan to serve the local HORECA and modern trade markets.

Today, Big Hill Lanka is a trusted supply partner to supermarkets, HORECA clients, wholesale markets, and modern trade outlets across Sri Lanka, with an export network spanning Canada, the USA, UAE, Qatar, Hong Kong, Thailand, and beyond.

**By the numbers:**

- Established **2019**
- **750+** HORECA clients
- **1,000+** large modern markets served
- **2,000+** modern markets served
- **40,000+** retailers reached
- **Islandwide** coverage

**Vision, Mission & Goal:**

- **Vision** — Continuously engage in market research and expand the product portfolio to meet evolving local and global demand.
- **Mission** — Deliver high-quality FMCG products to HORECA and modern trade markets, building lasting partnerships through reliable supply, competitive pricing, and outstanding service.
- **Goal** — Expand globally while leading locally, driving sustained growth through an innovating product portfolio and a fortified distribution network.
